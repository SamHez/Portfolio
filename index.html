about myself

I’m a 20-year-old software developer and designer based in Kampala, Uganda, with over seven years of experience building digital products. I got my first paying client at age 13, and since then, I’ve designed and developed over 100 websites for startups, NGOs, schools, restaurants, and small businesses.

I’ve also worked for two years as a Front-End Web Application Developer at Datatrack, an IT consultancy firm, where I contributed to government projects and a range of other enterprise solutions. This experience deepened my understanding of how large-scale systems are built and sharpened my ability to work in fast-paced, high-stakes environments.

My work blends UI/UX design, product thinking, and full-stack development to create digital experiences that are both functional and beautifully crafted. I believe great solutions begin with understanding the problem—and designing with people in mind.

When I’m not coding or designing, you’ll probably find me exploring new tech trends, reading, or gaming. But hey—enough about me. Scroll down and check out some of the work I’ve done.
